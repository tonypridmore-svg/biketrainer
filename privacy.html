Privacy policy

Bike Trainer · last updated 29 September 2026

Bike Trainer is a Windows app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own: the app's author never receives, sees or stores any of your data.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, rides, routes, workouts, training plan, segments and coach notes are stored in files on your computer. Sign-in tokens for the services you connect are kept in Windows Credential Manager on your computer, never in plain files.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Bike Trainer asks for permission to manage calendar events (the calendar.events scope). It uses that permission only to:

  • add your planned training sessions and finished rides to your calendar;
  • update or remove those entries when your plan or rides change;
  • find its own entries again, which are marked as the app's, including when you ask it to tidy away duplicate copies. To do that it looks through the events in the calendar and ignores every event it did not create. It keeps nothing about them.

Calendar data goes only between your computer and Google. It is not shared with anyone, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Bike Trainer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you choose to connect

  • Strava, to upload your rides and read your own activities and segments. Data goes only between your computer and Strava.
  • Google Gemini, for the training coach, using your own API key. Your training numbers, plan and questions are sent to Google's Gemini service to get the coach's answer.
  • Anthropic Claude, if you choose it for the training coach instead, using your own API key. Your training numbers, plan and questions are sent to Anthropic to get the coach's answer.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect any service inside the app at any time. You can also remove Bike Trainer's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Everything the app keeps is on your computer, so deleting the app's files deletes it.

Questions

Contact the developer at the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Bike Trainer.
