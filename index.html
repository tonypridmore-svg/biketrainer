Bike Trainer

A Windows app for riding an indoor smart trainer.

Bike Trainer controls a Bluetooth smart trainer for structured workouts and for riding real roads, with the gradient, your gears and ghosts of your past rides. It keeps a training plan, times you on your own segments, and uploads rides to Strava.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect it, Bike Trainer puts your planned sessions and finished rides in your Google Calendar, and keeps them up to date when the plan changes. It only adds, changes and removes the calendar entries it created itself.

Your data

Everything the app keeps stays on your own computer. Nothing is sent to the app's author. See the privacy policy for the details.

Bike Trainer is a personal project, shared privately. It is not affiliated with Google, Strava or any trainer maker.
